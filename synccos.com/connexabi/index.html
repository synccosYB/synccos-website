Synccos ConnexaBI Features

Comprehensive Contact & Company Database

Keep detailed records of hundreds of thousands of contacts and tens of thousands of companies, all within an intuitive and easy-to-use system.

Advanced Referral & Lead Management

Track and manage leads from first contact to closing, with powerful referral tracking to maximize your network's potential.

Custom Actions & Classification

Organize your contacts with custom actions and classifications, making it easy to target and manage different groups.

Group Creation & Mass Mailing

Create custom groups for mass mailing purposes, ensuring efficient and targeted communication for any occasion.

Integrated Texting & WhatsApp

Send individual or mass text messages and WhatsApp messages directly from the platform, with seamless integration and comprehensive tracking.

Robust Spam Management

Protect your communications with advanced spam detection, reporting, and compliance features.
